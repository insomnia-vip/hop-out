# HOP OUT launch kit

HOP OUT has one memorable idea: **a bag can look large while its exit is small**. Keep every launch post anchored to the product receipt, not generic frog lore.

## Positioning

- Product: exit-liquidity terminal.
- Enemy: the misleading last-price portfolio number.
- Mascot: the frog that measures the door before it jumps.
- Repeatable line: `BIG BAG. SMALL DOOR.`
- Question: `HOW BIG IS THE DOOR?`
- Proof: a live, timestamped 10/25/50/100% exit receipt.

## Credibility checklist

- Site and `/api/health` are live.
- The COPY sample completes from a fresh browser.
- GitHub Actions passes.
- README shows the formula and limitations.
- Site, profile, and pinned post use the same frog and line.
- Contract links are correct and checksum-verified.
- Launch post says `estimate`, never `guaranteed proceeds`.
- Holder Check requests only a public address; no signature, approval, network switch, or transaction request is introduced.

## What not to claim

Do not call HOP OUT an audited router, an executable quote, a guarantee, or protection from loss. Do not imply affiliation with Robinhood, Pons, or DexScreener. The product is useful precisely because its labels are honest.
